# Privacy Policy — FlixTV Pro

Updated October 21, 2026.

FlixTV Pro is a media player. It does not sell television subscriptions or provide a commercial catalog of channels, films, or series. Content and access authorization are the responsibility of the provider selected by the user. Where a demonstration is identified in the app, it uses technical samples only.

## Information used

To connect to the selected service, the app uses the server address, username, and password entered by the user. These are sent to the selected server for authentication, catalog requests, and playback. That server, and image and media servers, also receive technical data required for the connection, including the IP address and network requests. Provider HTTP connections do not offer the transport protection of HTTPS.

The app requests a configuration service to retrieve the available server list. That service receives the normal technical information associated with a network connection. Xtream credentials are intended for the server selected on the login screen.

Profiles, favorites, viewing progress, settings, and login information are stored on the device to support automatic login and continued use. Android/Fire OS credentials are protected using Android Keystore keys. Vega uses private app storage. Web-based versions, including Samsung Tizen, use the app's local storage; we do not claim that this storage has FlixTV-specific encryption.

Where available, voice search is started by the user and processed by the device recognition service. FlixTV uses the returned text to search the catalog and does not retain voice recordings. The platform voice service's permissions and policies also apply.

FlixTV does not include behavioral advertising or its own usage analytics service. Operating system, store, provider, and hosting services follow their own policies.

## Purposes and sharing

Information is used to authenticate the requested access, display and play media, preserve preferences, and handle support requests. We do not sell personal data. Connections to the provider and to image and video hosts are necessary for the functionality requested by the user.

Contact controls open WhatsApp or display instructions and a QR code. If you start a conversation, the information you send is subject to WhatsApp's policy. Avoid sending passwords, identity documents, or customer information through support.

## Control and deletion

You can sign out and remove profiles using the available controls. Signing out does not necessarily erase all local favorites and history. To remove local data completely, use the device's app-data clearing option, where available, or uninstall the app. Platform-managed backups and restores follow device settings. Local data is otherwise retained to support continued use. Provider and hosting retention is governed by their own policies.

Requests regarding your provider account, catalog, or provider-held data must be addressed to that provider. For FlixTV questions or privacy requests, contact app.flixtvplus@gmail.com. Do not send your provider password.

## Children and parental controls

The app is not specifically directed at children. Content ratings, availability, and descriptions depend on the provider. Parental controls depend on that information and do not replace adult supervision.

## Security and changes

We use protections appropriate to the functions described, but no system or connection can guarantee absolute security. This policy may be updated to reflect app changes; the version date will appear on this page.
